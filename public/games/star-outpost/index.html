DROID

Human war-robot legion · The Invaders

Cheap, mass-produced combat robots under remote human control. Individually fragile, but they win through sheer numbers and relentless pressure.

  • Style: Swarm & attrition
  • Strength: Low cost, fast production, fast units
  • Weakness: Low HP, no self-repair
Signature: Recycle — dead units refund Platinum

CREATURE

Alien warrior race · The Defenders

Physically dominant alien warriors wielding heavy plasma weaponry. Few in number and expensive, but each one hits like an artillery strike.

  • Style: Elite firepower
  • Strength: High HP & damage, natural regeneration
  • Weakness: Costly, slow to mass, Lithium-hungry
Signature: Natural regeneration — units heal out of combat

HIVE

Feral native swarm · The Infestation

A mindless organic swarm already infesting this world — the humans never knew it was here. Cheap and fast, it out-produces everything from its Hive Cluster.

  • Style: Fast swarm production
  • Strength: Dual production at the base, cheap & fast units
  • Weakness: Fragile bodies, weak per unit
Signature: Rapid Broods — the Hive Cluster produces two units at once
