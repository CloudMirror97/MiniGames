BARKHAM

A LOST DOG'S WAY HOME
They left you at the edge of Barkham — the fenced district where people drop the pets they stopped wanting. Strays run it now.
Every night you slip back in, dig through what the humans threw away, and look for a gap in the fence.

Whatever you carry out is yours. Whatever you drop, stays.
A raccoon with a cart knows the way out — and he will tell you, one errand at a time.
