How to Play

WASD / Arrow Keys: Move  |   Mouse: Aim & Fire
Space: Dodge Roll (invincibility frames)
1 / 2 / 3: Switch Weapon Slot (carry up to 3)  |  R: Reload  |  C: Use Active Item
I: Passive Item List  |  ESC: Pause
Passive items: carry up to 2, press Q / E to swap when full
Defeat all enemies to unlock the door
Boss rooms require a dedicated Boss Key (guaranteed once per floor) · Treasure Vaults require a separate key
Buy items with gold at the shop — stand near an item and press [F]
F is also used to descend the stairs after defeating a floor boss
Left stick: Move  |  Right stick: Aim & Fire (hold to keep firing)
